As an Assistant Professor, I currently serve as Director of the [**Observatory of South-East Tunisian companies**](https://foha2001.github.io/observatoire/theme/index.html) and a member of the Technology Transfer Office at the [**University of Gabès**](http://www.univgb.rnu.tn/Fr/accueil_46_3). I have been actively engaged in academia since joining the [**University of Tunis**](https://www.utunis.rnu.tn/fr) in 1998. After earning my PhD in Finance in 2010, I accumulated more than fourteen years of full-time teaching experience. I also coordinate the Master’s programs in Financial Engineering at the Higher Institute of Management of Gabès. My research interests span financial markets, risk management, data science, and corporate finance—areas that are essential both to regional economic development and to the advancement of academic research in finance.
